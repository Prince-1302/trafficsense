What TrafficSense Does

Predict
AI Congestion Forecasting

Historical and real-time data fused to generate congestion forecasts up to 90 minutes ahead — zone by zone, event by event. Officers act before the jam forms.

Deploy
Smart Officer Deployment

AI-recommended manpower assignments and barricading plans, pushed directly to field officers' devices. Zero guesswork, maximum coverage per zone.

Divert
Automated Route Diversion

Smart alternative route suggestions generated automatically based on live incident classification. Reduces incident resolution time by up to 40%.

Learn
Post-Event Intelligence

Post-event analysis loops back into the model — every incident makes the system smarter. Continuous learning from 8,200+ logged events across 12 zones.

Accident

Public Event
